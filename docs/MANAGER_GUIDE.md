# Manager guide

The salesman owes the business for the products billed to him. Record each real event once. A deposit is a movement of existing business money, not another collection.

## Start the day

Sign in with your own login. Confirm the date at the top. “DAY CLOSED” means posting is frozen. Book balances come from records; a bank book balance is not a live bank balance. Check unsaved drafts and pending online claims.

## Move between screens

Dashboard shortcuts and sidebar entries open the same destinations. **Receive Money** opens the payment section, **Record Expense** opens **Other Money**, and **Deposit / Transfer** opens **Cash & Bank**. Choose the particular transaction there. **New bill** and **Daily Close** open full-page entry screens from either location. Entry, review, statements, online verification and saved drafts use the main workspace with a **Back** button. Save a draft before leaving an unfinished entry. Navigation waits while a posting is in progress; retry failures on the same page.

## Make a bill

Salesman and Date are together at the top; today is selected automatically. Rows show Product, Quantity, Selling Unit, Rate / Basis, Discount and the server-calculated Amount. Rate/basis edits affect this bill only. Discount can be ₹ for the whole line or %, selected explicitly; leave it blank for none. A manager's price/discount edits need owner review unless the owner enabled those permissions. Such a bill offers **Send for owner review** and has no financial effect until approved. Notes and paper references are under optional details. Saved drafts preserve these edits.

Choose **New bill**, select the salesman, enter quantities and use Tab to move along. Leave unused products blank. Check the unit, conversion and rate basis. Enter cash actually received and any online claim separately if payment accompanies the bill. Choose **Review entry**, check previous balance, this bill, confirmed payments, pending claims and new balance, then **Confirm & save**. Online claims do not reduce dues until verified. The saved bill and each receipt are separate linked records.

**Save draft** does not post anything. **Saved drafts** resumes your own drafts. Prices/balances are checked again before posting. A draft that was changed elsewhere conflicts rather than overwriting the other tab.

## Receive an instalment or advance

Use **Receive Money** without creating another bill. Select the payer, actual receiving account, method and amount. Use **Record split payment** for cash plus online; the legs remain distinguishable and commit atomically. Oldest dues settle first, and overpayment remains the same salesman's advance. It is not another sale.

## Verify an online payment

In **Receive Money**, review the pending queue. Check the actual receiving-account statement/evidence. Enter its unique bank transaction identity and describe what you checked. Confirm once. A rejected claim needs a reason and has no money effect. A confirmed receipt cannot later be rejected; ask the owner for a reversal. A screenshot alone does not confirm anything.

## Return goods and refund money

Choose **Record linked return**, select the original bill line and actual returned quantity, and explain the reason. The original price applies, and earlier returns reduce what is still returnable. A return is a credit, not a cash payout. A refund is a separate owner-approved completed money movement against available credit. Manager refund actions create requests only.

## Expenses, salary and loans

Under **Other Money**, choose the real type. For an expense, enter category, total, payee/description and actual payment legs; the legs must add up to the total. Salary shows the full expense, any staff-advance setoff and only the actual net money paid. Staff advances and loan principal are separate from expenses. Owner funding is not sales. Money handed to the owner while still business-owned is a custody transfer; choose an owner withdrawal only for actual personal use.

## Deposit or transfer

Use **Cash & Bank**. A confirmed business transfer decreases one account and increases the other. A bank deposit handed over decreases cash and enters transit. Later use **Resolve deposit** for each credited or physically returned portion. Bank credit and returned cash together cannot exceed the deposit. Deposits never settle salesman dues again.

## Count cash and close

Choose **Close today**. Review activity, pending online claims and deposits. Count each physical cash account separately and enter its amount. Explain any difference. The app retains book cash and the unresolved variance; it does not invent an expense or reset tomorrow's opening. The owner can reopen with a reason, retaining earlier snapshots as stale. Never bypass a closed date by changing your computer clock.

## Statements, printing and errors

**Salesmen & Khata Book** shows each person's trade history. Use its date-range/print action for a statement with brought-forward balance. Loans remain separate. **Reports** supports filters and exact-decimal CSV of the displayed page. Spreadsheet formulas in text are neutralized. Printed operational records are not statutory tax invoices.

Wait for **Saved to business records**. If a request times out, leave the page open and retry the same action; the key protects against a second posting. Offline means not saved. If printing or evidence upload fails after saving, retry only printing/upload; do not enter the money again. Ask the owner for a linked reversal/correction rather than rewriting a posted amount.
